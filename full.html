Why v2

The eight kinds of invisible work we surface

V1 already tells the truth quietly ("2 reminders to accept sent"). V2 gives the work an author: Katie's team sent them, texted them, helped her pick, watched the tracking, paid everyone. Every number is a real admin count — the SMS log, the reminder log, the tracking events, the payout ledger — so the copy can only ever claim what actually happened. Each v2 line below is tagged with the kind of work it shows.

🔍
Casting & vetting

Profiles reviewed by hand, engagement checked, replacements sourced in 48h.

📱
Texts & nudges

The SMSes Katie's team sends — invite chases, check-ins, "you're up" pings. Counted from the SMS log.

⏰
Deadline reminders

Filming + posting deadlines texted to every creator so the brand never chases anyone.

🧴
Product picks

Creators choose their favorites from your selection — we set it up and double-check every order. Product collabs.

The brand sets each visit date — we remind creators before it and check in after. Local collabs.

Every draft reviewed against the brief before anything goes live.

Held from v1 + new in v2

Voice rules

Every line must pass these

  • Katie's team, never a machine. Work has a human author: "Katie's team texted 2 reminders", never "Reminders sent" or "Scanning…".
  • Never solo Katie in operational claims — "Katie's team" / "we". Katie's first person lives only in her signed cursive notes.
  • Receipts, not vibes. Every count is pulled from admin logs (SMS, reminders, tracking, payouts). If the count is zero, the line hides — no zeros, ever.
  • Close with "nothing for you to do." Every all-clear closer reassures + gives an honest horizon ("first delivery lands Thursday").
  • One amber exception: when the brand does owe a step (ship an order, review matches, say thanks), say it's the only step that's theirs.
  • Only knowable facts — tracking events, creator confirmations relayed by Katie's team, public posts, deadlines we set. No feelings, no off-platform scenes.
  • Count stages, never "all/last X" unless literally true. Declines are reassurance — the replacement story, never a dead end.
  • Views only >1k · quotes only >50 likes · top post = likes + comments · no link metrics, ever.
  • Vague-but-honest timing for creator-controlled steps ("usually within 48h"); real dates only for what we control or observe.
  • SMS lines only reference opted-in creators (portal checkbox = the legal opt-in). Counts come from the same log, so this is automatic.
  • Personal beats operational. Lead with the hands-on relationship ("individually texting each creator"), not task volume. Never sound like a struggling operation. (Katie, Aug 13)
  • No pool numbers at the invite stage ("214 profiles") and no engagement metrics before posts exist. (Katie, Aug 13)
  • Submit deadlines are not go-live promises. Say "you should start seeing content next week", never "drafts due Sunday" to the brand. (Katie, Aug 13)
  • Speed receipts only when true ("took less than 24 hours") — highlight pace when it's fast, say nothing when it isn't.
  • Creators are they/them in every line — never "she/her". Applies to kept-v1 strings too when they ship. (Julia, Aug 13)
  • Visits belong to the brand — creators email the brand, the brand sets the date. Our verbs: remind, track, check in — never "coordinate". (Julia, Aug 13)
  • Creators pick products on their own — we set up the selection and double-check orders; no "helped her pick". (Julia, Aug 13)
  • Benable-review mode: the brand never sees first cuts — closers stay all-clear until posts go live. (Julia, Aug 13)

Aug 13 · Julia × Katie review

The Katie pass — what we want, what we're careful about

Katie's read on v2, distilled from the call. The big steer: lead with the personal, hands-on relationship — not the volume of operational tasks. The tracker should make brands feel cared for, never like they're watching a struggling operation.

♥ Lead with — the language Katie wants

  • Personalized, individual touch: "6 personalized emails" · "individually texting each creator to make sure they see their invite ASAP".
  • Vetting the brand cares about: reviewed against your brief + confirmed open to gifted products.
  • Warm picks: creators "choose their favorite products from your selection" — we double-check everything runs smoothly.
  • Shipping comms: we tell each creator when their product ships and lands — and that it's time to film; tracking shared so they can plan their week.
  • Pre-delivery readiness: "we confirmed they're ready to film and know the campaign requirements."
  • Individual deadline dates by text — each creator reminded of their date.
  • Local: "Share the visit date and we'll handle reminding creators" · "First creator visits tomorrow — we've sent them a reminder."
  • Replacements: already sourcing — "we'll ping you ASAP."
  • Follow-ups, neutral: "If any follow-ups are needed, Katie's team will be on it."
⚠️ Never sayWhy
"Reminders to unresponsive creators"Implies we're struggling to get commitments. Reminder lines stay neutral — "still deciding".
Pool sizes at the invite stage ("214 profiles")Brands have no context — reads as "that's the whole pool" and implies later campaigns get weaker creators. Numbers live in the sourcing phase, if anywhere.
"Engagement" metrics before posts existNot established yet — raises questions we don't want at this stage.
Submit deadlines as go-live promises ("10-day window", "drafts due Sunday")That's the submit deadline, not go-live. Say "you should start seeing content next week".
"Reshare the brief"Too passive — say "we confirmed they're ready to film and fulfill the campaign requirements".
"Don't worry, we handle everything"Over-promise. The neutral follow-ups line instead.
Decisions from the call: Days 4 and 5 combine where acceptances overlap outreach (they often happen the same day) · sourcing lines render only while sourcing is actually happening · four variant axes to finish: Product / Local × Katie-reviews / Brand-reviews × Multi-location / Single location (the multi-location axis is new — not yet in this doc) · Julia finishes the full draft solo, then one final Katie review.

Before this ships

For Katie — settled on Aug 13 vs. still open

Settled on the call: no pool numbers at the invite stage · no engagement metrics before posts exist · reminder lines stay neutral ("still deciding", never "unresponsive") · follow-ups line is neutral ("if any follow-ups are needed, Katie's team will be on it") · submit deadlines never shown as go-live · days 4 + 5 combine · pre-delivery readiness check is claimable ("ready to film and knows the campaign requirements") · new variant axis to build: multi-location vs single location.

The copy can only claim what admin captures — check these

  1. Exact SMS counts: comfortable showing brands real numbers ("9 texts this week", "41 texts" at wrap)? They read straight from the SMS log — if a week is quiet the line hides (no zeros).
  2. "Texted" wording & consent: counts must only cover opted-in creators (the portal checkbox is the legal opt-in; Tony is confirming the manual-text carve-out). OK to name SMS as the channel, or should copy say "reminders" without the channel?
  3. Payments: DECIDED (Julia, Aug 13): payout lines are a paid-campaign fork — gifted campaigns (most of them) never show payment language. Still open: timing ("in motion" at day 22 → "paid" at wrap?) and the word — "paid", "payout closed", "payment sent".
  4. Product-pick help: is the helped-her-pick moment reliably logged in admin, so "Helped Nia and Maya pick their products" is always literally true? Same for visit-time coordination on local.
  5. Day-after check-ins: is checking in with a creator after her visit standard enough to claim every time ("we checked in with both after their visits")?
  6. Weekday visits: demo rows say Saturday/Sunday while the guidance says weekdays — which is true in practice? (📛 rows in the statuses chapter.)
  7. What's still invisible? This list covers texts, reminders, tracking, picks, visits, checks, payouts. What else does the team do weekly that admin could log and the tracker could say?
